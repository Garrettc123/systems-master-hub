   - Current data monetization efforts
   - Pain points
   - ROI goals
   - Timeline

3. **Demo** (10 min)
   - Live platform walkthrough
   - Asset registration
   - Bond issuance

4. **Use Case Mapping** (7 min)

   - Valuation estimate
   - Projected ROI
   - Implementation timeline

5. **Close** (3 min)
   - Pricing options
   - Next steps
   - Decision timeline

"Would you prefer to launch with Starter or Growth tier? Starter lets you test with 10 assets; Growth gives you unlimited."
